The Day

Every charter is private and planned around you.

We don't sell set packages, and you never share the boat with another group. Tell us the occasion — a sunset cruise, a harbor tour, an afternoon at anchor — and we plan the trip around it.

An experienced captain handles the boat, the navigation, and the weather calls. All you do is show up at the dock.

How It Works

Every charter is quoted individually.

There's no published rate card. The date, the hours, the size of your group, and what you have in mind all shape the price, so we prepare a quote for each trip.

01

Write to us

Send the form below, or email us directly. A date, a headcount, and a rough idea of the day is plenty to start.

02

Get your quote

We reply by email with a plan for the trip and a firm price for it. Ask questions, adjust, confirm.

03

Step aboard

Meet us at Palmer Point Marina in Cos Cob. Your captain will be ready at the dock.

We charter May 1 through November 1, weather permitting.
